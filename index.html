Profile

From regulatory genomics
to testable hypotheses

I'm a computational biologist with a PhD in Computational Systems Biology, working on cancer regulatory genomics, 3D genome regulation and integrative multi-omics.

In Dr Anita Göndör's group at Karolinska Institutet I study how a distal CTCF-binding site and long-range enhancer contacts shape MYC expression — combining nascent transcription (GRO-seq), chromatin accessibility (ATAC-seq), protein occupancy (CUT&RUN), chromatin contacts (4C-seq) and RNA-seq to prioritise hypotheses that the wet lab then tests. In our recent preprint, a single CTCF site in the CCAT1 super-enhancer turns out to act as a switch that pushes cells towards low or high MYC. I also lead the analysis of a clinical snRNA-seq cohort and analyse cancer whole-genome data for focal amplifications and ecDNA.

During my PhD I designed and published two original gene-prioritisation algorithms (chaos-game/multifractal and moment-of-inertia tensor), now available as tested, open-source Python packages. I care about reproducible analysis — Git, Nextflow/Snakemake, containers and Linux HPC — and about explaining results clearly to experimental colleagues and students.

What's next

I want to understand how cancer cells use their genomes flexibly — through changes in gene regulation, genome structure and cell state — to grow, adapt and resist therapy. My aim is to combine large-scale genomic, epigenomic and single-cell data with rigorous computational methods to turn these mechanisms into testable hypotheses and, ultimately, into biomarkers and therapeutic opportunities. Along the way, I want to keep building robust, reproducible analysis — from method development to clinical cohorts — in teams where data analysis helps decide what to test, target or develop next.

Research Focus

What I work on

Four connected areas: how cancer genes are regulated, how cancer genomes are rearranged, how cell types differ between patients, and the methods that tie it together.

Gene Regulation & 3D Genomics

How distal CTCF sites and enhancers control MYC, and how that control creates cell-to-cell variability in oncogene expression.

Cancer Genomes & ecDNA

Reconstructing focal amplifications — ecDNA, breakage-fusion-bridge and complex amplicons — from WGS with AmpliconSuite and nf-core/circdna, starting with public data from the MYCN-amplified LAN-5 neuroblastoma line.

Single-Cell Transcriptomics

Multi-donor sc/snRNA-seq with Scanpy and Seurat: QC, ambient-RNA and doublet removal, Harmony integration, annotation, and cell-type-specific differential testing.

Computational Methods

Original alignment-free gene-prioritisation algorithms, network biology and WGCNA, and statistical modelling of high-dimensional cancer-cohort data (TCGA/GEO).
